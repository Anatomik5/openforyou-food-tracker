**Full description (≤ 4000):**
```
OpenForYou Food Tracker is a simple, fast and completely free food diary. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no account — your diary stays on your phone.

SCAN AND LOG IN SECONDS
• Barcode scanner (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E) with a confirmation card: check the nutrition facts, choose the portion and add it
• Batch mode: scan several products in a row, adjust the weight of each one and log everything at once

• Calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates with clear progress bars and the amount left for the day

• Day view plus 7-day and 30-day statistics with your daily goal and average macro split
• Frequent and recent products for logging in two taps
• Search your saved products or the Open Food Facts database, or create your own products (also supplements and homemade food)

GOALS THAT FIT YOU
• Built-in calculator: sex, age, height, weight, body type, activity and goal (weight loss, maintenance, muscle gain, low carb)
• Edit your daily goals manually at any time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline-first: all diary entries, weight and settings are stored only on your device
• The camera is used only to read barcodes — images are never saved or sent
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics
• Export your diary as CSV whenever you like

NICE TO USE
• Light, Dark, Space and Summer themes — all free
• Available in English, German and Russian
• One-handed friendly layout with swipe navigation

Internet is needed only to look up a product that is not on your device yet. Product data: Open Food Facts (ODbL).

Calorie and nutrient values are estimates; the app does not replace advice from a doctor or dietitian.
```

## Data safety
Declared by the app's code (verify the final build before submitting):

- **Data stored only on the device, never sent to the developer:** diary, weight/height/age/sex, goals, settings. This is **not** "collected" under Play's definition → do **not** tick Health info / Fitness info / Personal info.
- **The only network traffic** is HTTPS requests to the public Open Food Facts API containing the barcode or the search text typed by the user (plus ordinary network metadata). No identifiers, no account.
- **Photos/videos:** camera frames are processed in memory only for barcode reading, never stored or sent → not collected.

Suggested answers:
1. *Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?* — The developer collects nothing. To stay conservative about the search text sent to Open Food Facts, you may answer **Yes** and declare only:
   - **App activity → In-app search history** · Collected: yes · Shared: no (public product lookup, not shared for the third party's own purposes) · Processed ephemerally: no · Required or optional: **Optional** (user-initiated search) · Purpose: **App functionality**.
   If you decide the lookup is covered by Play's exemptions, answer **No**. Check the current Data safety help text in Play Console and choose the option you are comfortable with.
2. *Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?* — **Yes** (HTTPS only).
3. *Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?* — Data is only on the device; users delete it in-app or by uninstalling / clearing storage. If the form requires a choice for the search-history item above, state that the developer holds no data.
4. *Data shared with third parties:* **None**. *Security practices:* data encrypted in transit; no independent security review.
5. *Families policy:* not applicable (not directed to children).

## Health apps declaration
- **Does your app have health features?** Yes.
- **Categories to tick** (choose those offered in the form): **Nutrition and weight management**. Do **not** tick medical-device, clinical, disease-management, Health Connect, or sensor-based categories — the app has none of them.
- **Medical device / diagnoses / treatment:** No. The app gives general calorie/nutrient estimates; an in-app note says it does not replace a doctor or dietitian (Profile → calculator).
- **Health Connect / Health Services:** not used.
- Keep the store text free of medical claims (no "treat", "cure", "diagnose", no guaranteed weight-loss results) — the texts above follow this.

## Permissions declaration
Only `CAMERA` (barcode scanning) and normal permissions `INTERNET`, `VIBRATE` (haptic feedback). `RECORD_AUDIO`, `READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` are explicitly blocked in `app.json`.

## Release checklist
1. Publish `PRIVACY.md` (public URL) and paste it into Play Console.
2. Upload `assets/icon.png` (resize to 512×512 for the store icon), `store-assets/feature-graphic.png` (1024×500) and at least 2 phone screenshots per language.
3. Closed testing: new personal developer accounts must run a closed test with the required number of testers for the required period (currently 12 testers for 14 days — check the current rule) before applying for Production.
4. Build: `eas build -p android --profile preview` (APK for your phone) → `eas build -p android --profile production` (AAB for Play). Increase `android.versionCode` in `app.json` for every upload.
